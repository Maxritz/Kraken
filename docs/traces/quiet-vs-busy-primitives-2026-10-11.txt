=============================================================================

this file (the audit's own limitation: the quiet arms drift 20-30% between
sessions and the alloc row halved since the previous one).

Before this build the gate printed `launch floor (copy_act n=32): -24.00 us per
call` (and -21.50 us in an earlier one): with the switch at 2 the per-call
subtraction is the difference of two single measurements and can come out
negative, which the tool printed as a cost.

########## 2. the busy-queue arm, and the version that did not work ##########

First attempt, using time_calls like the other arms (a batch mean minus a cold
call), depth 64:

  primitives, queued (64 x copy_act n=32 ahead of every call):
    record -12.82 us marginal | copy 1.55 us marginal [empty queue: record 29.52 | copy 2.88]

A NEGATIVE marginal cost for a real operation. time_calls subtracts one cold call
from the mean, and for a host-side enqueue the cold call is the expensive one, so
the subtraction removes more than the quantity. The copy arm is fine because a
kernel's cost scales with the queue; the record's does not. The arm was replaced
with the direct one in section 1: fill the queue with 512 copies, time 64 calls,
one drain per block, median of 8 blocks.
