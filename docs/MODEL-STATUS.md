## What the numbers say

* **Dense models are at the wall.** 19 of them, 75-97% compute-engine busy. Nothing on the MoE path is involved; the kernels are the limit and the remaining headroom is kernel work, not tiering.
* **MoE is the outlier and tiering does not close it.** Qwen3-MoE-4x0.6B, whose whole routed bank fits in VRAM, runs 79.6 tok/s at 31% busy. laguna-xs2, whose bank is 18.9 GiB, runs 5.6 tok/s at 6.8% busy while reading 166 MiB per token from the file: the decode step is host-bound on expert bring-in, not device-bound.
* **That is the number Strata's design attacks.** They compute the non-resident experts on the CPU while the GPU works, so the device is never idle waiting for an expert. Our device sits 93% idle during laguna decode for exactly that reason.

## Caveats

* laguna-xs2 **fails coherence**: greedy output differs run to run (see the open bug). Its row is a speed number only.
* Spark_one's historical drift was at step 12 of a longer run; the 16-token gate passes, so that is "passes the gate", not "fixed".
* The coherence column covers the 25 models small enough for the scalar reference. Laguna-S-2.1 (3 shards), Qwen3.8-27B x3, ornith/Tiel-Coder 35B, Qwen3-30B-A3B and Qwen2.5-Coder-32B are runnable but untested: the CPU reference needs minutes per token core on files that size.
